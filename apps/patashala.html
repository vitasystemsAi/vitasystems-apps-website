Patashala is a comprehensive School ERP designed to simplify and automate school operations. Built for administrators, teachers, staff, parents and transport teams.

Capabilities

Powerful modules for complete school management

Every critical school function in one centralized, easy-to-use platform.

  • Student Management

    Manage admissions, student profiles, classes, sections and academic years seamlessly.

  • Staff Management

    Maintain staff details, roles, departments and manage access with role-based control.

  • Fee & Payment Management

    Fee collection, multiple and partial payments, pending tracking and detailed payment reports.

  • Transport Management

    Route and vehicle management, trip allocation, driver management and real-time transport visibility.

  • Timetable Management

    Create, manage and publish timetables with easy updates and export options.

  • Daycare Management

    Enable/disable daycare, manage students and daycare fee collections efficiently.

  • Notifications

    Centralized notifications across all modules for better communication and transparency.

  • Dashboards & Reports

    Insightful dashboards and detailed reports for better decision making.

  • Refund Management

    Refund requests, policy management and tracking made simple.

  • Role-Based Access

    Secure access for administrators, teachers, staff, parents and transport teams.
