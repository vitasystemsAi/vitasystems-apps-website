Vita Application · Dental Clinic Management

SKDCM

Sri Konduri Dental Clinic Management System

One Platform. Complete Dental Clinic Management.

SKDCM is an end-to-end dental clinic platform covering patients, chair-side charting, lab, pharmacy, billing, face attendance and payroll. Built for Sri Konduri Dental Clinic & Implant Center, Sathupally.

Open SKDCM skdc.konduridental.com Back to hub

Capabilities

Powerful modules for complete clinic management

Every critical dental clinic function in one centralized, easy-to-use platform.

  • Reception & Patients

    Manage patient registration, profiles, records and front-desk workflows seamlessly.

  • Clinical & Odontogram

    Chair-side charting with a full odontogram for accurate, real-time clinical records.

  • Online Appointment Booking

    Let patients book, reschedule and track appointments online without front-desk calls.

  • OP & Visit Management

    Track outpatient visits, treatment history and follow-ups across every consultation.

  • Lab Management

    Coordinate lab work orders, case tracking and turnaround with in-house or external labs.

  • Pharmacy Management

    Manage prescriptions, dispensing and pharmacy inventory linked directly to patient visits.

  • Billing & Payments

    Treatment billing, multiple and partial payments, and detailed financial reporting.

  • Face Attendance

    Face-recognition based staff attendance for accurate, contactless time tracking.

  • Payroll Management

    Automate staff payroll, calculated directly from attendance and role-based pay rules.

Benefits for every stakeholder

  • For Administrators

  • For Patients
